Business Model & Payment Gateway Use Case

Geni AI Global Hub is a digital artificial intelligence ecosystem providing powerful AI-driven creative generation services. Our customers utilize platform tools to craft professional AI images, dynamic videos, pristine audio, expressive voice content, and unique digital assets.

We operate on a secure digital credit architecture. Clients purchase flexible digital credit packages (AI Coins and App Currency) to unlock and execute specialized AI generation workflows seamlessly.

Our payment gateway integration is dedicated strictly to processing customer transactions for digital AI services and credit bundles (AI Coins and App Currency). There are no recurring subscription fees; all purchases are transparent, one-time credit packages.

How Customers Use Our Platform (Complete Journey)

  1. Users visit Geni AI Global Hub to explore our comprehensive suite of AI features and digital credit packages.
  2. Users select their preferred credit bundle based on their generation requirements.
  3. Users proceed to secure checkout and select their payment option.
  4. Payments are processed securely via the integrated payment gateway, including Rapid Gateway where applicable.
  5. Upon instant payment confirmation, purchased credits are immediately deposited into the user's wallet.
  6. Users spend these credits to generate advanced AI images, videos, audio, or speech outputs.

Payment Gateway & Digital Delivery Purpose

Rapid Gateway functions as the core payment processor for customer transactions on Geni AI Global Hub, utilized exclusively for digital credits (AI Coins and App Currency). No physical items or merchandise are sold. All purchased credits and generated content are transmitted electronically and instantly to the customer profile following payment verification.
