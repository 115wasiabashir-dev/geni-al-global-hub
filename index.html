Business Model & Payment Gateway Use Case

Geni AI Global Hub is a digital artificial intelligence platform that provides AI-powered creative generation services. Customers can use our digital tools to create AI-generated images, videos, audio, voice content, and other creative digital outputs.

Our business operates through a digital credit model. Customers purchase available digital credit packages (AI Coins and App Currency) and use those credits within the platform to access AI-powered generation features.

The intended use of the payment gateway is to accept customer payments for our digital AI services and digital credit packages (AI Coins and App Currency).

There are no subscription plans. Customers only purchase one-time credit packages.

How Customers Use Our Platform (Complete Customer Journey)

  1. Customers visit Geni AI Global Hub and browse available AI services and digital credit packages (AI Coins and App Currency).
  2. Customers select the desired credit package.
  3. Customers proceed to checkout and select their preferred payment option.
  4. Payment is processed through the available payment gateway, including Rapid Gateway where applicable.
  5. After successful payment confirmation, the purchased digital credits (AI Coins or App Currency) are instantly credited to the customer’s account wallet.
  6. Customers use their purchased credits inside the platform to generate AI images, videos, audio, or voice content.
  7. Generated content is delivered digitally and made available inside the customer’s account dashboard.

Payment Gateway Purpose

Rapid Gateway is used as a payment processing method for customer purchases on Geni AI Global Hub. The gateway is intended solely for payments related to our digital AI services and digital credit packages (AI Coins and App Currency).

No physical products are sold. All payments are for digital credits only.

Digital Delivery

Geni AI Global Hub provides digital services only. There is no physical shipping or physical product delivery. Purchased credits are delivered electronically and instantly to the customer’s account after successful payment confirmation.
