Business Model & Payment Gateway Use Case

Geni AI Global Hub is a digital artificial intelligence platform that provides AI-powered creative generation services. Customers can use our digital tools to create AI-generated images, videos, audio, voice content, and other creative digital outputs.

Our business operates through a digital credit and service model. Customers purchase available digital credit/coin packages or applicable subscription services and use those credits within the platform to access AI-powered generation features.

The intended use of the payment gateway is to accept customer payments for our digital AI services, digital credit/coin packages, and applicable digital subscriptions.

Payments made through Rapid Gateway are therefore directly related to the purchase and use of Geni AI Global Hub's digital AI services. No physical products are sold through this platform.

How Customers Use Our Platform

  1. Customers visit Geni AI Global Hub and select an available AI service or digital credit package.
  2. Customers select their preferred payment option at checkout.
  3. Payment is processed through the available payment gateway, including Rapid Gateway where applicable.
  4. After successful payment confirmation, the purchased digital credits, coins, subscription, or applicable digital service is made available to the customer's account.
  5. Customers use their purchased credits or service access to generate AI-powered digital content through the platform.

Payment Gateway Purpose

Rapid Gateway is used as a payment processing method for customer purchases on Geni AI Global Hub.

The gateway is intended for payments related to our AI digital services, digital credits/coins, and applicable subscription services.

Customers do not purchase physical merchandise through Rapid Gateway. Payments are for digital services and digital products provided through the Geni AI Global Hub platform.

Digital Delivery

Geni AI Global Hub provides digital services only. There is no physical shipping or physical product delivery.

Following successful payment confirmation, purchased credits, coins, subscriptions, or applicable digital services are delivered electronically through the customer's Geni AI Global Hub account.

AI-generated content is produced digitally through the platform and is made available to the customer through their account or applicable digital service interface.
