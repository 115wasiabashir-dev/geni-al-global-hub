Refund Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

Geni AI Global Hub provides digital products and services only. Digital credits (AI Coins), App Currency, and AI generation services are delivered instantly to the customer’s account upon successful payment confirmation.

1. General Policy

Due to the instantaneous digital nature of our products and services, all purchases are generally non-refundable once the digital credits or services have been successfully delivered to the customer’s account.

2. Cases Where Refunds or Corrections May Be Considered

Refund or correction requests may be reviewed only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Technical payment failure (payment amount was successfully deducted but digital credits or services were not credited to the account)
  • Duplicate charges for the exact same transaction
  • Verified system error on our side that prevented proper delivery of purchased credits

3. How to Request a Review

If you believe you are eligible under the cases above, please contact our support team within 7 days of the transaction date and provide the following information:

  • Full Transaction ID / Payment reference number
  • Date and exact amount of the transaction
  • Screenshot or proof of the payment confirmation
  • Clear description of the issue experienced

After receiving the request, we will verify the transaction with the payment gateway. If the issue is confirmed, we will either correct the credit delivery or process a refund (as applicable) through the original payment method where possible.

Email: basmallahnoor@gmail.com

5. Processing Time

Valid and verified requests will be reviewed and processed within a reasonable timeframe after complete verification. Customers will be informed of the outcome via email.
