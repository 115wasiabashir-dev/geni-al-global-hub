Delete Your Geni AI Account

To permanently delete your Geni AI account and all associated data, please enter the email address linked to your account and submit your request.

Your profile, coins, currency balance, rewards, and other associated account data will be permanently deleted. Any remaining coins or currency will be lost and cannot be restored.

This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

If you delete your account, you will need to create a new account if you decide to use Geni AI again in the future.
