Geni AI Global Hub - Business Model & Use Case

1. Business Explanation

Geni AI Global Hub is a multi-modal artificial intelligence platform offering AI text, image, and video generation services and coin packages to users.

2. Service Delivery Model & Customer Journey

Users purchase AI service packages or coin bundles through the app using Rapid Gateway. Once the payment is verified, the purchased coins or services are instantly credited and delivered digitally inside the user's account dashboard.

3. Refund Policy

Since our services involve instant digital delivery and AI generation credits, refunds are only applicable in case of technical transaction failures where the payment was deducted but coins/services were not credited to the user account.

  • Refund requests must be submitted within 7 days of the transaction.
