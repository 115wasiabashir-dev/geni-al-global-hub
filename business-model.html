2. Products & Services Offered

We offer the following digital products and services:

  • AI Image Generation
  • AI Video Generation
  • Text to Image
  • Image to Video
  • Text to Video
  • AI Audio Generation
  • AI Voice Generation
  • Creative AI Tools
  • Digital Credit Packages (AI Coins)
  • Digital App Currency Packages

Difference between AI Coins and App Currency

  • AI Coins: Used primarily for AI video generation and other coin-based AI features.
  • App Currency: Used primarily for AI image generation and other currency-based creative features.

Both are digital credits purchased by customers and spent inside the platform to access AI generation services. No physical products are sold.

4. Complete Customer Journey

The complete customer journey on Geni AI Global Hub is as follows:

  1. Customer visits the Geni AI Global Hub website or application.
  2. Customer browses available AI services and digital credit packages (AI Coins and App Currency).
  3. Customer selects the desired package.
  4. Customer proceeds to checkout and chooses a preferred payment method.
  5. Payment is securely processed through the available payment gateway (including Rapid Gateway where applicable).
  6. Upon successful payment confirmation, the purchased digital credits (AI Coins / App Currency) are instantly credited to the customer’s account wallet.
  7. Customer uses the purchased credits inside the platform to generate AI images, videos, audio, or voice content.
  8. Generated content is delivered digitally and made available inside the customer’s account dashboard. No physical shipping is involved.

5. Intended Use of Payment Gateway

The payment gateway is used solely to accept customer payments for the following digital products and services:

  • Digital AI generation services
  • Digital credit packages (AI Coins)
  • Digital App Currency packages

Important: No physical merchandise of any kind is sold through this platform. All payments are strictly for digital credits and digital AI services only. There are no subscription plans.

6. Refund Policy Summary

Because digital credits are delivered instantly upon successful payment, refunds are only available in limited cases of technical failure or duplicate charges. Full details are available on our Refund Policy page.
