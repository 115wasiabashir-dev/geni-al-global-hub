Geni AI Global Hub – Business Model & Use Case

1. Business Explanation

Geni AI Global Hub is a multi-modal artificial intelligence platform offering AI text, image, video, audio, and voice generation services along with digital coin and currency packages to users worldwide.

2. Service Delivery Model & Complete Customer Journey

  1. User creates an account or logs into Geni AI Global Hub.
  2. User browses available AI services and digital credit/coin packages.
  3. User selects the desired package and proceeds to checkout.
  4. Payment is processed securely through the available payment gateway (including Rapid Gateway where applicable).
  5. After successful payment confirmation, the purchased digital credits, coins, or subscription are instantly credited to the user’s account wallet.
  6. User spends the credits inside the platform to generate AI images, videos, audio, or voice content.
  7. Generated content is delivered digitally and made available inside the user’s account dashboard.

All services are 100% digital. There is no physical product or shipping involved.

3. Intended Use of Payment Gateway

The payment gateway is used solely to accept customer payments for:

  • Digital AI generation services
  • Digital credit and coin packages
  • Digital subscriptions

No physical merchandise is sold.

4. Refund Policy Summary

Because credits are delivered instantly upon successful payment, refunds are only available in cases of technical failure (payment deducted but credits not received) or duplicate charges. Requests must be submitted within 7 days of the transaction.
